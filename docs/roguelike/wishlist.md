## Boons (Sages)

### Tulin (Sage of Wind)

- Double jump, mobility focused
- Long range wind slice via attack, like elden ring's stormblade

### Sidon (Sage of Water)
- Easier, dependable builds
- Bubble shield, blocks damage
- Enemies drop heals and extra poe on death
- Rapid stream or bubbles for knockback

### Yunobo (Sage of Fire)
- Damage over time focus, needs upgrades and synergies to melt everyone
- Attacks stack fire
- Fire resistance

### Riju (Sage of Lightning)
- Crowd control, not a lot of single target damage
- Stuns, chain lightning

### Mineru (Sage of Spirit)

- Zonai weapons get some kind of bonus
- Also sheika?
